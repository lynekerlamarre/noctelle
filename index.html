    Questions

    Before you choose.

    The things people ask us most, answered straight.

    Will it actually last?

    Yes. Our parfums are 22 to 28 percent perfume oil. Most designer eau de toilettes sit under 15. On most skin you'll get 8 to 12 hours, and longer on clothes.

    What's the difference between your parfums and colognes?

    The parfums are deeper and warmer, made for evenings. The colognes are brighter and fresher, but they're cologne intense at 15 to 18 percent oil, so they still last 6 to 8 hours instead of the usual two.

    Are these for men or women?

    Both. We don't split the collection by gender. Wear the one that smells like you.

    What if none of them are right for me?

    Then you've spent $38 instead of $185, and you keep the set. Unopened full bottles can be returned within 30 days for a full refund.

    I have sensitive skin. Can I still wear them?

    Every formula meets IFRA safety standards and we publish the full ingredient list for each scent. If your skin is reactive, spray on your clothes or scarf. The trail is just as strong.

    How does shipping work?

    The discovery set ships in two business days. Full bottles ship free on orders over $150.
