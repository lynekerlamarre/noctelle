    Questions

    Before you buy.

    The things people ask us most, answered straight.

    Are these really authentic?

    Yes. Every bottle is the original product, sealed in the brand's own box. You can check the batch code printed on the box at CheckFresh, and if anything is ever not authentic, you get a full refund.

    Why are your prices lower than the department store?

    We buy in bulk from wholesale distributors and don't pay for a shop on a high street. The savings go into the price, not the bottle.

    How fast will it arrive?

    Orders ship within 1 to 2 business days with tracking. Perfume ships by ground, so most orders arrive in 3 to 7 days.

    Can I return it?

    Unopened bottles in their sealed box can be returned within 14 days. Opened bottles can't be resold, so we can't take those back unless something is wrong with them.

    Is paying on the site safe?

    Checkout runs on Stripe, the payment system behind millions of online stores. You can pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, and we never see or store your card number.

    You don't have the one I want. Can you get it?

    Often, yes. Tell us what you're after in the form below and we'll let you know if we can source it and for how much.
